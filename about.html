About the Songs

“I was praying, and my sweet Jesus came, placing Himself near me in order to pray together with me; even more, His intelligence was reflected in mine, and I prayed with His; His voice echoed in mine, and I prayed with His word. But who can say the boundless effects of this prayer?” Servant of God Luisa Piccarreta, Book of Heaven, Vol. 15, April 25, 1923

As you pray or sing these rounds, you become like Esther before the King’s throne, asking for mercy upon humanity.

“This creature in Our Will is the New Esther wanting to rescue her peoples. O! how Happy We are to keep her always with Us in Our Will because, through her, We feel more inclined to use Mercy, to concede Graces, to Forgive the most obstinate sinners, and to lessen the pains of the purging souls. Poor daughter! She has a thought for everyone, and a pain similar to Ours, seeing the human family swimming in Our Will without recognizing It—living in the middle of enemies in the most wretched misery.” Servant of God Luisa Piccarreta, Book of Heaven, Vol. 36, October 30, 1938

Hello! My name is Kristina Joy. I am an oil painter from Minnesota with a deep love for the Divine Will and the writings of the Book of Heaven by Luisa Piccarreta.

When I first began praying the Rounds of the Divine Will, it was a real struggle. Little by little, they became easier as I understood them more. But it was when God gave me the idea to set the Rounds to music that I began to LOVE praying them.

Together with the Holy Spirit (it is mostly Him) and the assistance of AI technology, I create these Round Songs to help myself and others pray the Rounds of the Divine Will, joining our prayers with Jesus as we long for the coming of His Kingdom on earth. Technology can be used for evil or for good, and I want to use the tools available today for God’s glory.

After hearing them, a number of people asked for a hard copy of the songs. So I created this website, where you can order the songs on CD.

How Do I Create the Songs?

First, God gives me an idea for a song. Then I do research from different sources, like the Bible, the Book of Heaven, and The Hours of the Passion. Often a phrase, a line, or a theme will pop up in my head.

For example, in the song “Lean on me Jesus,” the very last part is when Judas kisses Jesus. God gave me the idea of the children of the Divine Will kissing Jesus in that same kiss, so that Jesus would be relieved, consoled, and loved in that very act of betrayal.

Then I work with AI to turn my ideas into lyrics. It often suggests lines and helps with the rhythm and rhyme, and I edit and correct them until the words say what I believe God wants them to say.

Lastly, I use AI to bring the music to life.

Music is an incredible vehicle to pray the Rounds.

But the true power of these songs comes not from the music. It comes from YOU, as you immerse yourself in God’s Divine Will and pray them in your heart or with your lips. United with Jesus, your voice becomes one with His. These songs are simply a tool to help us pray the Rounds.

Why Does the App Cost Money?

I wish I could give the app away for free! But making it comes with real costs:

  • Apple charges a yearly fee just to keep an app in the App Store, and keeps part of every purchase.
  • The music tools I use to create the songs have monthly fees.
  • The website name (singdivinewillrounds.com) has to be renewed every year.
  • The app needs ongoing updates so it keeps working well when Apple releases new iPhones and new iPhone software, plus fixes and new features.

Your subscription helps cover these costs and lets me keep making new songs, which show up in the app on their own.

If the app is not in your budget, that is okay! Every song is still free to download on the Little Children in the Divine Will website.
