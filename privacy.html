Privacy Policy

Sing Divine Will Rounds app, by Kristina Joy Nelson. Last updated November 5, 2026.

What the app collects

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking or analytics.

What stays on your phone

Your favorite songs, text size, repeat and sleep settings, and any songs you save for offline listening are kept only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Internet use

The app downloads the song list, artwork, and songs from singdivinewillrounds.com. Like any website, that server may briefly record basic technical information such as an IP address to deliver files. This is not used to identify you.

Purchases

Subscriptions and gifts are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app never sees your payment details.

Notifications

If you turn on “Tell Me About New Songs” in Settings, the app asks permission to send a notice when new songs are added. You can turn this off anytime in the app or in your iPhone Settings.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
